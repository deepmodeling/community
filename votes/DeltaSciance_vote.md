# A Vote for New Projects: DeltaScience

## Prososal
**DeltaScience** is an open evaluation platform for large models and AI agents on real scientific research tasks — and, as a DeepModeling community project, the open home of its public task sets, verifier tooling, and evaluation archives: a "living benchmark" that measures whether AI can deliver scientific artifacts that survive reproduction and quantitative acceptance, rather than whether it can answer exam-style questions.

**Background.** Released in November 2026 by the AI for Science Institute, Beijing, jointly with DP Technology, Zhejiang University, The University of Tokyo, the University of Cambridge, the Institute of Physics (CAS), and Beihang University, DeltaScience (https://deltascience.bohrium.com) is a "living benchmark" of 65 non-public real-world tasks from university labs and frontline R&D teams in drug discovery, materials, energy, and engineering (7 discipline categories, 35 subcategories, four task types). Deliverables are scored in [0, 1] by hidden, physically isolated verifier sandboxes against real physical or engineering metrics — a clean exit code never counts as a pass. The first official evaluation ran 11 frontier Model + Harness combinations × 65 tasks × 5 rounds (3,575 formal runs; 10,000+ sandbox executions including pre-checks and re-tests): the top combination scores 61.6/100, swapping the Harness gains more than swapping the model, 70.4% of task×combo cells never reach a full score in five rounds, and 61.4% of zero-score traces are "code ran but failed scientific acceptance" — scientific judgment, not tool execution, is the dividing line.

**A note on governance.** DeltaScience operates under a three-layer decoupled governance model: DeltaScience Benchmark — non-profit and academically neutral (task sets never sold, never used for model training, never commercially monetized) — owns acceptance standards and monthly versioning; Playground runs year-round submissions and authoring-competition incentives; Bohrium provides the isolated sandbox infrastructure without interfering in task review. With fixed versioned task sets for longitudinal δ re-testing and monthly new tasks scored independently, DeltaScience is a natural complement to the DeepModeling ecosystem: it brings real, machine-verifiable scientific problems into the community, and returns open tasks, tools, and citable credit to contributors.
