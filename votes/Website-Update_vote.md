# A Vote for Updating the DeepModeling Project Landscape

## Proposal

The 2025 revision of the Manifesto broadened the community's scope from "scientific computing" to "scientific research", but the official website still uses four categories inherited from the community's early days. None of them can accommodate the new directions the Manifesto names — SciAssess (AI for literature analysis) and Uni-Lab-OS (AI for experimental science) had no place on the website at all.

This proposal brings the project landscape in line with the Manifesto, and adjusts the project roster in the same step.

**1. Restructuring into Read / Compute / Experiment.** The landscape is reorganized into three layers following the Manifesto's research loop:

- **Read** — Literature, Data & Knowledge
- **Compute** — Electronic Structure Solvers / Models, Atomistic & Molecular Solvers / Models, Phase Field & FEM Solvers, Mesoscopic & Macroscale Models, Data & Workflows, Infrastructure & Interfaces
- **Experiment** — Experiment Automation

Within Compute, projects are annotated by technical route (machine learning vs. physical modeling), the two approaches the Manifesto calls the most important applications of computing. The navigation menu groups the ten categories into six, to keep projects easy to find. The deprecated maturity labels (Mature / Incubating / Sandbox) are no longer shown.

**2. Adding twelve projects.** Uni-Lab-OS (Experiment); sciencepedia, SciAssess, AI4S-agent-tools (Read); deepmd-gnn, unimol_tools, LAMBench, dpnegf, CrystalFormer-CSP (Compute — models); ABACUS-agent-tools, AIS-Square, dargs (Compute — infrastructure).

**3. Removing two projects.** *ADMP* has had no real commits since 2022-02-15 and its capability is fully absorbed by DMFF (shared submodule, shared documentation, shared author on DMFF's JCTC 2023 paper); no functionality is lost. *cemo* was abandoned within a week of creation — five commits, four of them LICENSE/README/gitattributes, no releases. Both GitHub repositories are kept and will be marked as migrated or deprecated.

**4. Preview.** The updated site is deployed and browsable at https://test.deepmodeling.com/

## Deadline

The vote will be open for at least 7 days unless there is an objection.
